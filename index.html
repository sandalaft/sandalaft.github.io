Section 3: UK inflation

Inflation is the United Kingdom is measured by the Office for National Statistics (ONS) using the Consumer Prices Index (CPI). The CPI measures the average change in prices of a basket of goods and services over time. The ONS collects data on prices from various sources, including retailers, service providers, and online platforms.
